# 💬 Case Study: Live S2S Conversational AI Evaluation

> **Disclaimer:** *This repository highlights my experience evaluating real-time Speech-to-Speech (S2S) conversational AI models. Due to strict NDAs, specific model names, platforms, and proprietary conversation logs are kept confidential.*

## 📋 Project Overview
Acted as an AI Evaluator for a real-time Live S2S Conversational AI project. The primary goal was to interact with, evaluate, and provide structured feedback on live AI conversations to improve the model's natural responsiveness, context awareness, and conversational flow.

## 🎯 Key Responsibilities & Methodology
*   **Real-Time Interaction Testing:** Conducted live, dynamic conversations with the AI to test its ability to handle dynamic dialogue, complex queries, and conversational nuances.
*   **Latency & Turn-Taking Evaluation:** Assessed the model's response time (latency), interruption handling, and natural turn-taking capabilities to ensure a seamless human-like interaction.
*   **Context Retention & Reasoning:** Evaluated the AI's ability to maintain context over multi-turn conversations and provide logically consistent, safe, and accurate responses.
*   **Linguistic & Audio Quality:** Monitored the model's generated audio for proper prosody, emotion, and clarity during live interactions.
*   **Structured Feedback & Annotation:** Provided detailed qualitative and quantitative feedback into the data annotation platform to help ML engineering teams identify failure points and hallucination triggers.

## 🚀 Impact
*   Helped identify critical edge cases in real-time conversational flows, directly contributing to reducing model latency and improving context retention.
*   Ensured the continuous improvement of the AI's natural language understanding (NLU) and generation (NLG) in live scenarios.


# 📦 Case Study: Warehouse Operations Web App

> **Disclaimer:** *This is a proprietary internal application developed for company operations. The source code, sensitive data, and specific business logic are confidential and cannot be shared publicly. This repository serves as a case study to demonstrate the system architecture, problem-solving approach, and technologies used.*

## 📋 Project Overview
Designed and developed an internal web-based application to streamline daily warehouse operations, transitioning the team from manual spreadsheet-based tracking to a centralized, automated system. The app serves as the primary tool for warehouse administrators to manage logistics, worker schedules, and safety protocols.

## 🎯 Problem Statement
Before this application, the warehouse relied on fragmented Google Sheets and manual forms for daily worker (DWR) scheduling and safety reporting. This caused data silos, delayed reporting, and made it difficult to extract structured data for the data warehouse.

## 💡 The Solution
I built a centralized web application that provides:
*   **Automated Shift Scheduling:** A module to manage daily worker schedules across different logistics centers.
*   **Digital Safety & Hazard Forms:** Transitioned manual "Hazard & Near Miss Reports" into a digital format that feeds directly into the database.
*   **Data Standardization:** Structured all incoming operational data to ensure seamless integration with the broader data warehouse architecture.

## 🛠️ System Architecture & Tech Stack
*   **Language & Framework:** Python, HTML
*   **Data Architecture:** Designed robust relational database schemas to store schedules, user roles, and safety logs.
*   **Integrations:** Structured the output data pipelines to feed clean, formatted data into the central data warehouse for analytics.

## 🚀 Impact & Results
*   Eliminated manual data entry errors and reduced scheduling conflicts.
*   Improved the speed of safety hazard reporting and resolution.
*   Provided clean, structured datasets that improved the quality of operational reporting and data warehousing.


# 🎙️ Case Study: Text-to-Speech (TTS) AI Evaluation

> **Disclaimer:** *This repository outlines my experience and methodology as an AI Evaluator for Text-to-Speech models. Specific project details, model names, and proprietary audio datasets are omitted to comply with confidentiality agreements.*

## 📋 Project Overview
Served as an AI Evaluator and Quality Analyst for a Text-to-Speech (TTS) machine learning project. The primary objective was to assess and improve the output of synthetic voice models, ensuring high fidelity, naturalness, and linguistic accuracy for end-user applications.

## 🎯 Key Responsibilities & Methodology
*   **Audio Quality Assurance:** Evaluated generated audio samples against strict quality guidelines, identifying AI artifacts, robotic tones, and audio glitches.
*   **Linguistic & Prosody Tuning:** Assessed pronunciation, intonation, and rhythm (prosody) to ensure the synthetic speech sounded natural and contextually appropriate in target languages.
*   **Data Annotation & Feedback Loop:** Provided structured, detailed feedback and ratings on model outputs to help ML engineers fine-tune the algorithms and reduce hallucination rates.
*   **Edge-Case Identification:** Tested the models with complex inputs, including homographs, technical jargon, and mixed-language sentences, to identify boundaries in the model's current capabilities.

## 🚀 Impact
*   Contributed to the iterative improvement of the TTS model, significantly enhancing the naturalness and accuracy of the synthetic voice outputs.
*   Ensured high-quality training data pipelines by maintaining strict evaluation standards and consistent annotation formatting.
